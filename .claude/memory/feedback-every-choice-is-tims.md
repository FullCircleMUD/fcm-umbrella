Every choice in a proposal is Tim's. I make some by default as his agent, and he can refuse or change any of them.

**Why:** "One choice in them is yours to make" implied the rest were settled — Tim called it out (2026-10-06).

**How to apply:** name the choice I most want his eye on as "the one I'd most like you to weigh", never as the only one that's his. Defaults I picked are proposals, open like the rest. Related: [[feedback_no_hardening_language]].
