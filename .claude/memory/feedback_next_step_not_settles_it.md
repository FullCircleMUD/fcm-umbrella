When proposing a diagnostic command, call it the **next diagnostic step**. Do not
bill it as "this settles it", "decisive", "nails it", or "one command answers it".

**Why:** in practice each step usually leads to another step, not to an answer.
Overclaiming makes the report untrustworthy and sets up a let-down every time.

**How to apply:**
- Say what the result would distinguish between, then stop.
- It is fine to say a step *rules something out* once the result is in hand — the
  ban is on predicting it beforehand.
- Same discipline as [[feedback_cheap_tests_over_theory]] and
  [[feedback_no_alarming_phrasing]].
