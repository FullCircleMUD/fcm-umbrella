In an understanding phase, explain the mechanism and stop. No option tables, no recommendation, no "do you see another option?" until Tim says he is ready to decide.

**Why:** offering options early railroads him into a quick decision before the problem, and what is needed, are fully understood (Tim, 2026-10-07, on the shard-transfer ownership work).

**How to apply:** if he is restating or probing the problem, reflect it back accurately and name what is still not understood. Options wait for "I'm ready to decide" or an equivalent.

**A survey is for understanding the structure, not solving it.** When Tim asks for a survey or a starting map, report how things fit together and stop. Per-item mechanics, targeting rules, edge cases and "questions" for every item wait until that item comes up, one at a time, together (Tim, 2026-10-12, on the spells survey).

**A short or ambiguous remark gets a question, not an interpretation.** Never restate it as a specific design ("reading that as X") and then build numbers or fixes on X — that narrows his thinking for him. Ask what he means (Tim, 2026-10-15, on Jev for encounters).
