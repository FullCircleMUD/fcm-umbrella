Nothing exists in the world unless a player crafts it. Stated by Tim 2026-10-02 while mapping
recipes, rejecting "training weapons as a starting kit or sold by NPC trainers".

**Why:** the game is a player-led economy; items are owned on chain and every item has to come
through a crafter.

**How to apply:** when a recipe problem tempts a non-crafted supply route (starter kits, vendor
stock, NPC handouts), rule it out and solve it inside crafting — fewer item types, or one recipe
with a choice of output. Related: [[feedback-engine-not-content]].
