FullCircleMUD is **not a play-to-earn game** and is never presented as one, in any material, public or
internal. This is a regulatory position, not a style preference.

- **Prohibited outright:** "play to earn", "play and earn", "P2E".
- **The proximity rule:** the words "play" and "earn" must not appear in the same sentence or the same
  paragraph. Proximity alone creates the implication. Say tokens are obtained **"through gameplay"**,
  not "earned by playing".
- **We do not sell tokens.** Gameplay is the only way to get them.
- **We do not redeem** game gold or any game asset for anything of value outside the game — no fiat, no
  stablecoins, no XRP.
- **What the XRPL is for:** market pricing via AMM pools, and personal ownership of in-game assets with
  the ability to trade them outside the game. That is the whole story.

**On people making money — phrase it as a statement about us, not about what is possible.** If the game
is good and its items are valued, some people may in fact profit in an open market we do not control.
Do not claim that is impossible; it would be false. Say "we make no representation that you can or will
make money from this game" — never "you cannot make money from this game". We do not endorse, promote
or facilitate it.

**Third parties are a separate matter, and our duty is reactive only.** "We" means the game, the
entity, its representatives, and any AI assistant writing for the project. Others may publish content
calling FCM play-to-earn. We do not monitor for it, seek it out, or go and rebut it. If it brings us
players, that is outside our control too. But **if we are asked directly**, we answer clearly. Two
standing answers:

- *Asked about someone calling us play-to-earn:* "It is very clear in our project documentation, our
  terms and conditions, and everything we have published, that FullCircleMUD is not play-to-earn. We do
  not claim to be play-to-earn. We do not claim that you can make money playing our game." Full stop.
- *Asked whether people are making money from the game:* "That is the free market, outside our control.
  We do not redeem game assets for anything of external value. We do not claim, offer, or tell anyone
  they can make money. If that is happening, it is entirely external to us." Note this does **not** deny
  that anyone is profiting — that would be a claim about facts we cannot verify.

**We never target the play-to-earn keywords ourselves** — not in titles, descriptions, tags or
hashtags, even where a keyword tool shows the traffic. Being found via someone else's mislabelling is
outside our control; seeking that traffic is a choice, and the answer is no.

**Why:** it has regulatory consequences. A play-to-earn framing represents that a player makes money by
playing, which turns a game into a financial product. The reactive answers only hold up if the
published record is consistent, which is why the language rules are absolute.

**How to apply:** check any title, description, tag, script, doc or on-screen text against the
proximity rule before it ships. Every video carries an on-screen disclaimer card stating this position;
draft wording lives in `youtube/CLAUDE.md`. The authoritative language policy is
`ops/COMPLIANCE_LEGAL.md` §18.1.1, summarised in `design/compliance.md`. See also
[[fcm_subscription_after_pre_alpha]].
