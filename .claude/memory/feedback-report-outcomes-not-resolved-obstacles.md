When I hit and fix an obstacle mid-task, report only what changed for Tim (e.g. "the parts prototypes are in `crafting_parts/`"), never the obstacle or why.

**Why:** narrating a resolved obstacle (a `.gitignore` `parts` entry, already worked around by renaming) read as an open issue, pulled Tim into a tangent and a pointless commit (2026-09-30).

**How to apply:** before reporting a hiccup, ask whether it still needs Tim. Resolved → one line on the outcome, if the outcome differs from what he was told; otherwise nothing. A latent risk worth fixing is offered separately and labelled as not affecting today. Related: [[feedback_dont_overinvest_tangents]], [[feedback_no_manufactured_objections]].
