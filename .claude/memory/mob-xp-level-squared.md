Current practice: a mob's `experience_points_value` starts from the rule of thumb level² × 10 for its level equivalent. The formula is a guide and a good starting point; the builder has permission to tweak it up or down as they see fit.

**Why:** a starting curve keeps rules consistent without making any value fixed. Tim, 2026-10-24: nothing here is a rule or canon.

**How to apply:** take the formula as the first value for a new spawn rule, and adjust it where the builder has a reason. Levelling pace overall is balanced on the experience level table. Related: [[feedback-mob-stats-are-build-decisions]].
