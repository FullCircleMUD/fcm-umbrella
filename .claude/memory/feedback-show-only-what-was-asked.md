**Why:** in the 2026-10-10 proxy-token review he repeatedly had to say "I want you doing what I ask you to do"; the add-ons pushed decisions he hadn't raised.

**How to apply:** the reply is the view itself. If something looks wrong, wait until he asks. Relates to [[feedback_question_is_not_instruction]] and [[feedback_terse_confirmations]].
