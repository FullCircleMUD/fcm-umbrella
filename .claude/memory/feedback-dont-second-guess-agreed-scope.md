Once Tim has stated the scope of a chunk of work, execute it. Don't close with "one thing to confirm
before I start" about something already decided, and don't answer a yes/no question with a
multi-section breakdown.

**Why:** re-confirming settled scope reads as not listening, and an unasked-for inventory buries the
one-word answer he wanted. He'll ask for the breakdown when he wants it — he did, one turn later.

**How to apply:** yes/no question → "Yes." Scope stated → do it, report after. Genuine forks go in
the reply *while* the design is open, not after he's closed it. Related:
[[feedback_terse_confirmations]], [[feedback_question_is_not_instruction]],
[[feedback_dont_overinvest_tangents]].
