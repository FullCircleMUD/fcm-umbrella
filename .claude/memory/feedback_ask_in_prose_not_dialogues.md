Don't use the multiple-choice question dialogue (AskUserQuestion) to put decisions to Tim. Ask in
prose instead, in the normal reply.

**Why:** the dialogue leaves no space for nuance — the answer has to be one of my framings or an
outright rejection. Tim's actual answers routinely sit outside the options I'd have listed (e.g.
"add the hub room *and* a down-only exit to the Broken Crown", "let's discuss that one").

**How to apply:** state the finding and the open question in the reply text, give a recommendation,
and let the answer come back in Tim's own words. Related: [[feedback_terse_confirmations]],
[[feedback_stop_on_each_problem]].
