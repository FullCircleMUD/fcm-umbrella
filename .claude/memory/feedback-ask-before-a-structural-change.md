When an agreed edit turns out to need a structural change to work, name it and ask before doing it — even when it is the better solution.

**Why:** Tim agreed a flag and one archive at the end of the experience level-up method (2026-09-30). The flag only works if the recursion becomes a loop, and I rewrote it to a loop, along with the plan, tests and docs, without asking. He liked the loop, but it was not what had been discussed.

**How to apply:** say "this needs X to work — OK?" and wait. Related: [[feedback_cooperative_design_loop]], [[feedback-never-refactor-dependencies-unasked]].
