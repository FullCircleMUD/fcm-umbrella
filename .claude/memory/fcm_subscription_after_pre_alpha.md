FullCircleMUD is **free to play during pre-alpha**. The intent is that at a more developed stage it
moves to a **regular monthly subscription fee**.

**Why it matters:** it is part of the required disclaimer on every video, so players are told up front
rather than discovering it later. It also reinforces that the game is a game with a normal business
model, not a financial product — see [[not_play_to_earn]].

**How to apply:** state it in the future tense — the subscription is intended, not live. Do not quote a
price; none has been set. `[TBD — the amount, and the stage at which it starts.]`
