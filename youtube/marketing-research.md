# Marketing research — keywords, hashtags and titles

Keyword, hashtag and title candidates for the FCM YouTube channel. Nothing decided — material for
discussion, per [CLAUDE.md § Nothing here is locked in](CLAUDE.md).

Volume and fit ratings are estimates. Confirm the volumes in a keyword tool before betting titles on
them.

## The hard rule

**Never "play to earn", "play and earn" or "P2E"** — and the words "play" and "earn" never share a
sentence or paragraph. Not in titles, descriptions, tags or on camera. Say tokens are obtained
**"through gameplay"**. Full rule in [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) and [design/compliance.md](../design/compliance.md).

This costs us the biggest traffic term in the category. Accepted — that traffic is people looking for
income, not players.

## The mechanic that decides everything

Nobody searches "XRP Ledger MMORPG" in any volume. But hundreds of thousands watch XRP content weekly,
and **suggested videos, not search, are where our traffic comes from.**

So keywords are there to tell YouTube our video belongs beside XRP content. We are chasing the same
*viewer* as the big XRP channels, not the same *query* — they own price and news, and we are not in
that race.

Three consequences:
- Use the ecosystem's exact words in the title and the first two description lines.
- **Say them out loud on camera.** YouTube indexes the transcript, and Tim narrates anyway.
- Carry both "XRP Ledger" and "XRPL" — outsiders search one, the community says the other.

## The formula — a starting convention

A working shape for each video, not a rule. Pick **one primary** and **one secondary**, build the title
around them, and let the rest fill in. Expect to bend it once we see what actually performs.

| Surface | Usually carries |
|---|---|
| **Title** | A primary, plus the secondary leading that video |
| **Description** | The primaries, and a handful of secondaries worked in naturally |
| **Hashtags** | The three visible ones covering the primaries; the rest pick up secondaries |
| **Spoken on camera** | The primaries said out loud — YouTube indexes the transcript |

Two things worth holding to:

- **Rotate the leading secondary** where we can, so the channel spreads across the pool rather than
  stacking on one term.
- **The title still has to earn a click.** Hitting the keywords is the floor, not the goal. If a title
  reads like a keyword list, rewrite it — a video nobody clicks ranks for nothing.

The compliance rule above is the one genuine exception to all this: that one is not a convention and
does not bend.

## Hashtags

- **Long form, the three shown above the title:** `#XRPL #XRPLedger #Web3Gaming`
- **Long form, rest of the set:** `#XRP #NFTGaming #BlockchainGaming #MMORPG #IndieGame #AINPC #Xaman #GameDev`
- **Shorts — three to five only:** `#Shorts #XRPL #Web3Gaming` plus one that fits that short.

Both XRPL forms are spent on the visible three deliberately. Signalling "we are one of yours" to the
XRPL community beats a broader third tag.

**Skip the price hashtags** — `#XRPArmy`, `#HODL`, `#Altcoin`, `#XRPPrice`. They bring speculators, and
they sit badly next to a video saying this is not an investment. Tim's call.

**Mechanics:** the first three appear above the title and are the ones that matter. Over 15 and YouTube
ignores all of them. Hashtags are a small lever next to title and description.

```

[One sentence on what happens in this video.]

Play FullCircleMUD (pre-alpha): [staging URL]
Join the Discord: [Discord invite]

[Timestamps]

FullCircleMUD is a text-based MMORPG built on the XRP Ledger, where your items and
currency are real on-chain assets you own. It is pre-alpha, built by one developer, and
free to play in your browser. Not an investment — it is a game.

#XRPL #XRPLedger #Web3Gaming
[remaining hashtags]
```

The first two lines carry the keywords and the links sit above the fold. YouTube truncates the rest
behind "show more".
